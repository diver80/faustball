📖 Authentic Faustball (Fistball) Rules & Controls

Faustball is an athletic grass sport dating back to ancient Rome, standardized in Germany and Austria:

  • The 1-Bounce Rule: The ball is permitted to bounce on the grass once between consecutive touches. A second consecutive bounce without a hit awards a point to the opponent.
  • 3 Touches Max: Touch 1: Defense dig/reception -> Touch 2: High setter pass -> Touch 3: Spiking punch or strategic drive over the ribbon.
  • Center Ribbon ("Die Leine"): Suspended at 2.0m height with red/white stripes. Hitting the ribbon or passing below is an immediate fault.
  • Controls: ← → or A D to run • ↑ or W to Jump • ↓ or S to Dive ("Hechtbagger") • Space to Hit/Spike • Shift to unleash Special Ability!
